# Folio: the Scroll as an essay

Folio draws a Scroll as an essay in one scrolling column: beats of text, figures set after the beats that change them, and a figure that holds while the beats about it pass (#732, D-423). It is the reader for every `scroll-document.v2` (D-425). Status: **implemented**, unit-tested and smoke-tested on the web; the hold is checked on the Android emulator by `apps/client/maestro/folio.yaml`, and Android is otherwise #740.

## How a document becomes a screen

```
scroll-document.v2 ──readingModelOfV2──► ReadingModel ──► FolioReader
```

- `reading-model.ts` is what the reader draws: sections of blocks, each a **beat** (one unit of reading, named by the document's unit id), a **figure** (a picture state, with a control under it when it has one) or a **hold** (one figure and two or three beats, with the figure's state for each). The reader knows no document version.
- `from-v2.ts` makes one from a released `scroll-document.v2`. Every layout choice is made there, from the document alone, so the server sends nothing new. Its doc comment lists the rules. A stage whose kit this build does not draw (a stored revision of a kit since removed, which stays readable) is read as no stage: every beat is text and nothing is drawn, so the old revision never throws (`drawableStage`).
- `from-v3.ts` does the same for a `scroll-document.v3` (below); the reader needed no new idea for it beyond what the model now carries. The screens that name a unit to the server (Ask, Quick answer, Share a moment, Challenge, Keep, Report and the reading record) send a stop id today; a v3 beat id needs those contracts too.

## Reading a document v3 (#762)

Status: **implemented**, unit-tested (`from-v3.test.ts`, `beat-view.test.tsx`, `picture-control.test.tsx`, and a browser test for the keys and roles in `linked-text.web.test.tsx`). `readingModelOfV3` (`from-v3.ts`) turns a `scroll-document.v3` into the same `ReadingModel`; the pictures are drawn by the general renderer (`../picture/`), not a kit.

- **Sections.** Each section of the document is a section of the essay, under its title as a level-2 heading. A run of beats on one picture never crosses a section: each section starts afresh, so its first beat has no figure to keep and stands alone if it shows nothing.
- **Pictures and holds.** A beat that shows a picture has the picture after it. Beats in a row on one picture, two or more of them changing it, share a hold of at most three beats (`holds.ts`, the rule v2 follows too); a fourth starts a new figure, and showing another picture ends the run. A beat that shows nothing keeps the picture and state before it, so inside a hold it draws the figure it follows. Beats are named by the document's beat ids.
- **Linked phrases and the pulse.** A phrase a beat links to a part of its picture is an inline button, underlined in the colour its part is drawn in (warm, cool, or the page's link colour for a mark or a part with no tone), the words themselves staying in ink. It is reached by Tab and pressed by a tap, click, Enter or Space; assistive tech reads it as a button, "Show <phrase> in the picture". Pressing it emphasises that part in every picture that has it for 1.2 s (`pulse` in the reader, passed down as `pulsing` to the held figure too). It is emphasis, not movement, so reduced motion changes nothing. The plainer words (the ••• row Simpler, Shift + S) show with no links, as before (D-429).
- **Quote beats.** A quotation is set apart: a rule on its leading edge, the words in quotation marks and italic, then "— who, work, year" under it in the secondary ink. It is a labelled group ("Quotation from <who>"). Its phrases can be linked like any other beat's. Plainer words replace it, label and all, and carry no cite, since they are not the quotation.
- **Controls.** A beat with a control gets its figure to itself (never in a hold, since a control under a pinned figure would move as the text moves), with the control under the plate. The figure starts in the beat's state and the control changes what is drawn here, for this reader only: nothing is reported or stored (D-209). Every target is at least 44 px, the rows wrap at 320 px, and each control has a polite live region (`role="status"`) that says its sentence, which is also shown. The picture's Text chip still gives its words.
  - **Scrub.** Previous and Next, then one chip for each position, one chosen (a radio group named by the control's label). A chip is tapped to go there; Previous is off at the first position and Next at the last. Keys, with a chip focused: Right or Down for the next, Left or Up for the previous, Home and End for the first and last; the focus follows. Under it, the position's label and the picture's words for that state. A position the beat's state is not one of (a scrub that reveals more, started on the whole) is chosen by the first press. A scrub along items lights each item the same way, so a beat that has revealed only the first items still reveals each one as its chip is chosen (`lightItem`, D-438).
  - **Switch.** One toggle button, named by the control's label, pressed when the picture is in the switch's state; Enter or Space flips between the beat's state and the other. Its sentence is the picture's words for the state it is in.
  - **Pick.** One button for each item that has a note, none chosen at first (a radio group). Choosing lights that item (`lit` is just it) and shows and says its note; keys as the scrub's. A lit item is always revealed (`lightItem` in `@knowscroll/scroll-model`, the same rule the scrub along items uses): when the beat's `upTo` is below the item's place, the state raises `upTo` to include it, because an item `upTo` has not reached is not drawn and the chip would change nothing; with no `upTo` nothing changes (D-438).
  - **Tilt** is a v2 kit's control and stays in `KitFigureView`.
- **To sum up.** The document's takeaway is the end plate's "To sum up", and its limits aside is Small print. A v3 document has no picture of its whole journey, so the end plate shows none.

## The cover

A Scroll can be shown as a cover first and opened on tap (`essay-cover.tsx`, `EssayCover`). It shows the three lines the essay opens with, one picture, a Read (or Continue) button with the reading time, a "1 OF 3" place in the hand, and ways to the previous and next cover: two round arrows and a strip along the foot that names what the next move shows ("NEXT · kicker" over the next title, or "MORE COVERS" after the last).

- **The opening is shared.** `essay-opening.tsx` (`EssayOpening`) draws the kicker, title and dek for the essay and the cover alike, so the type is identical and opening a cover reads as the same page unfolding. The title is the one `h1`: the cover replaces the essay on screen.
- **The picture cannot be operated.** It is the Scroll's first stage state drawn on a plate like the essay's, with no "Text" chip and no control (no tilt slider). It is the part that gives way to the `height` the cover is given: shorter, then dropped when under about 140 px is left, as on a small phone or at large text. What remains scrolls inside the cover; no line of text is clipped.
- **Opening has one control.** A tap on the opening or the picture opens the Scroll for a pointer or a finger, but that area is not a focus stop or a screen-reader control: the Read button is the one way in for a keyboard.
- **It is the Scroll's page** (`scrollPageSurface`, the page palette), so Light and Dark match the essay it opens into. It moves nothing beyond what `useKitFigure` does, and nothing under reduced motion.

`/gallery-folio?covers=1` draws one hand of five labelled fixture covers (first, no stage, words only, Continue, last), each as tall as the window leaves it.

## How reading is measured

Each beat's wrapper is measured by the article's hooks (`../reading/`): IntersectionObserver on the web, layout and scroll offset on native. Only a band of the viewport counts (`readingBandOf`: from 42 % down to 12 % above the bottom), so a beat hidden behind a held figure is never visible and only a few beats count at once. The ids reported are stop ids, as the Stage reader's were; nothing a person does with a figure or a play block is reported. The stop being read (for Keep, Ask and Share a moment) is the last beat whose top has passed the top of the band; a kept place or a jump puts its beat there, so the stop opened is the stop being read.

Measured on 18 Nov in Chromium at 390 × 844 over the Seasons CI fixture and judged by the server's own `summariseReading` and `qualifyScrollReading`: a careful read (about 2½ minutes) qualified (coverage 1, q 0.94); a fast one (about a minute) did not (coverage 0.25); a ten-second skim did not (coverage 0).

## Looking at it locally

The gallery build has `/gallery-folio`, which draws the v2 CI fixtures with no backend: `?doc=` one of `seasons`, `nerve`, `starlight`, `blackHole`, `scaleChart`, `sortFork` (and the document v3 fixtures `fenceV3`, `seasonsV3`, `broadStreetV3`); `stop=` to open at a stop; `motion=reduce`; `scale=2`. It records what the reader reports in `window.__ksFolioGallery`.

```
pnpm --filter @knowscroll/client build:gallery
cd apps/client && node e2e/serve-export.mjs dist-gallery 4331   # then open http://127.0.0.1:4331/gallery-folio
```

In the app, open any v2 Scroll. Tests: `pnpm --filter @knowscroll/ui test` (unit), `e2e/folio-gallery.spec.ts` and `e2e/folio-reader-smoke.spec.ts` (browser).
