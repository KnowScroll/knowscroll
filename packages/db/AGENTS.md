# Persistence ownership

Append ordered numbered migrations. Never rewrite an applied migration or remove user history to get a test green. Coordinate shared schema changes before independent agents implement consumers. Tests use isolated databases. Ledger rows are source events; Accounts/Trace are rebuildable projections. Privacy lifecycle may erase payloads under a later explicit policy; append-only is not a promise to retain personal data forever.

ADR-0009 governs session hashing, expiry/revocation, ownership FKs and privacy epochs. ADR-0010 defines the bootstrap history-clear policy: authenticated universe lock, expected epoch, exact retry receipt, calling-session rollover, scoped FK-safe removal and monotonic revisions in one transaction. Do not retain deleted payloads in receipts or logs. Shared assets and unrelated universes survive. Full account deletion, backups and semantic reset have separate unresolved retention boundaries.

Read ADR-0012 before reasoning-runtime work: separate private execution and retained minimal accounting identities; universe-first locks; consumed dispatch intents are never replayable. A late old-epoch receipt may settle original accounting without private output authority. Exact history-clear receipt replay returns before any reasoning/accounting mutation. Migration 0004 and reasoning-storage.ts implement storage, history-clear erasure, restricted receipt append and bounded retention cleanup. Read docs/operations/reasoning-storage.md for caller transactions and remaining limits. Migration 0005 and the admission/reconciliation factories implement atomic development primitives. Read docs/operations/reasoning-runtime.md before consuming them: trusted resolver/context callbacks, immutable input/policy bindings, cumulative usage and no replay after ambiguous commit. J004 verifies synthetic process faults; product dispatch remains disabled. ADR-0015 below adds separate scheduled cleanup for safely withdrawn Jobs.

Migration 0006 and `reasoning-fairness.ts` implement internal durable fair admission. Read `docs/operations/reasoning-sql-fairness.md`: one probe transaction, universe-first locks, scheduler generation fencing, complete vector preflight, retained cross-epoch debt and immutable private ready rows. Shared scheduler resources lock before physical buckets; product dispatch remains disabled.

Migration 0007 and `reasoning-context.ts` implement internal session-bound direct Scroll compilation and concrete authority. Read `docs/operations/reasoning-context.md` and ADR-0014 before consuming it. Authenticate and compile on the same client/transaction; first compilation permanently binds that Job to its original session. Context locks precede scheduler/resources; post-resource rechecks acquire no new locks. Proposal/application and product dispatch remain absent.

Migration 0008/ADR-0015 define seven-day retirement only for safely withdrawn cancelled/expired Jobs. Read `docs/operations/reasoning-retirement.md`. Preserve the authoritative withdrawal clock, original live-lease requirement, immediate Clear History, and separate all-duties-closed accounting retention. The maintenance entrypoint is separate from projection; it must not release unknown liabilities, infer timestamps or broaden withdrawal authority.

ADR-0016/migration0009 add source-only explicit Ask facts. Read `docs/operations/explicit-asks.md` before consuming them. Authenticate on the same transaction, hold the universe lock, require the expected epoch before replay, preserve literal question bytes and original-session provenance, and let only Clear History erase the pair after epoch advance. The recorded_only receipt grants no queue/provider or V1 Keep-context authority.

ADR-0017/migration0010 add separate literal Ask context compilation. Read `docs/operations/reasoning-ask-context.md`. A recorded Ask grants no execution authority; compiler consumes only a separately authorized caller-supplied queued direct Job. Preserve original-session provenance, exact family routing and universe→session→Job→asset order. Post-resource rechecks acquire no new locks. Keep V1 stays Keep-only; retirement removes private bindings but preserves Ask source history until Clear.

ADR-0018/migration0011 extend withdrawal only to original-session-bound idle direct Jobs. Read the accepted contract before cancellation/expiry work. Preserve fenced live-worker withdrawal, exact original binding, universe→session→Job→children→fairness→bucket locks and lock-free final rechecks. Refund only proven not_sent before final idle-credit clamps; preserve unknown liabilities. Deadline expiry is based on the actual Job database deadline. Maintenance adds a bounded expiry lane without changing168-hour retirement or owner-process deployment.

ADR-0019/migration0012 add a separate database finish clock and168-hour maintenance for safely completed/failed private graphs. Read `docs/operations/reasoning-retirement.md`. Preserve the original withdrawn clock/guard, explicit mutual exclusion, live old lease and unchanged fence at terminalization, legacy-null refusal and post-wait full eligibility recheck. No completion/application consumer or provider authority is introduced. Unknown accounting/permits/debt survive private deletion; saved source history is not an erasure target.

ADR-0030/migration 0017 add the privacy lifecycle contract (pause, export, reset) as schema only — no route or `packages/db/src/privacy.ts` function yet. `universe.recording_paused_at` plus a `ledger_pause_guard` trigger make pause a real database refusal of new exposure/keep/ask rows, not an application promise; `privacy_recording_receipt`/`privacy_export_receipt`/`privacy_reset_receipt` follow `history_clear_receipt`'s exact-retry shape. Reset performs every Clear step (ADR-0010) plus revokes every device session for the universe, including the caller's — the one concrete way it is stronger than Clear. It does not touch `sign_in_token` (already immutable and 15-minute-capped by ADR-0026), the account/universe rows, or the generated-Reel supply chain (no `universe_id`, not personal data). Read ADR-0030 before implementing any of the three routes.

ADR-0031/migration 0026 add the semantic substrate (`packages/db/src/semantic/`). Read the ADR before changing it: knowledge rows are immutable; a bridge exists only through an admitted proposal decided by `packages/core/src/semantic/bridge-validator.ts`, with its read-set slice recorded; corrections propagate by revalidation and record every effect; universe lock before the substrate advisory lock; branch/feedback/universe proposals are private history erased by `erasePersonalHistory` before exposures.

ADR-0038/migration 0032 add background bridge inquiries (`reasoning-inquiries.ts`, `reasoning-inquiry-context.ts`, `reasoning-inquiry-execution.ts`). Consent is per universe and epoch and changes only with a recorded request; mail is written only by `applyDeltas` for a planet/region formed in the same transaction, with consent and while recording; the worker opens a due inquiry under the universe lock with fresh authority. The inquiry context family seals consent, recording, route, places, claims and pair disconnection and rechecks them at admission, dispatch and apply. Idle background Jobs are withdrawn by `withdrawIdleBackgroundJob` only after the inquiry records its cause. Clear/Reset erase inquiries before the atlas and semantic history.

ADR-0041/migration 0035 add model-written Scrolls (`semantic/model-scrolls.ts`). `source_material` is a page's private text, bound to its snapshot by hash; no route reads it, and it is shared library, so no privacy operation erases or exports it. `scroll_writing` decides each (material hash, request hash) once: admitted with its Scroll, or refused with reason codes and no model text. Admission is one transaction under the substrate lock; a source the substrate holds is reused only while its current snapshot has the same hash.

ADR-0046/migration 0040 add inventory (`inventory/`). A demand is written in the transaction that observed the need (a v3 feed decision on an exhausted live place, a continuation opened or offered into a concept with nothing unseen), never while paused, and decided by the pure Quartermaster under the universe lock, then the concept's supply lock, then the route's bucket row. Supply (`scroll_writing_route`, `scroll_material_candidate`, `supply_request`) is shared library naming no universe: no privacy operation erases or exports it, and a funded request holds one unit of its route's bucket until sent or settled unsent (`supply_request_budget`). Demands, waiters and bindings are private history: pause cancels open demands and waiters (never another universe's or a shared request), Clear/Reset erase them before places, bridges and exposures, export carries them. A correction that leaves a bound Scroll unsupported withdraws its bindings in the personal-model refresh (and so in the correction catch-up) and reopens the demand.
