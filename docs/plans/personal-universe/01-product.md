# Product: an evolving personal universe

Status: conversation baseline, with proposed measurement and unreviewed screens explicitly identified.

## Problem

I want the ease and visual appeal of scrolling, with ideas worth encountering. I want to follow something interesting as far as I feel like, move elsewhere just as easily, and gradually recognise a world that feels like mine. I should not have to take tests, answer onboarding questions or keep pressing Like to make that happen.

## Agreed experience

KnowScroll helps a person discover themselves through the evolving way they encounter and understand the world. It offers opportunities for self-recognition without announcing an interpretation of the person.

- Reels and interactive Scrolls are equal ways to encounter ideas. A person can prefer Reels for ML and Scrolls for history, and ask to switch the current explanation's format.
- Vertical exploration brings different subjects and perspectives. Horizontal exploration follows a thread: a real next part when available, or a related example, mechanism, implication or alternative perspective.
- Familiar Like, Save and Share controls keep effort low. Questions are available when the person chooses to ask, not required by the app. There are no compulsory quizzes or learning reports.
- Repeated, varied encounters gather into systems, planets and regions. A star organises a broad topic system; planets represent subtopics; regions represent finer areas. The worlds remain after an absence.
- Each universe has a recognisable visual language and its own materials, terrain, atmosphere and routes. Visual richness need not be a public ranking of how much someone consumed.
- Friends can visit deliberately shared places. A shared discussion room can sit in different locations on different people's maps. The moderator organises; clearly identified AI inhabitants can participate.
- Content feels like a well-written article or engaging Reel. Evidence and limitations shape the writing; research logs and recommendation rationales do not become the reading interface.
- A thread can reach a satisfying stopping point. There is no missed-reward pressure or destruction caused by time away.

## Success metric — proposed, not approved

Measure the percentage of new people who voluntarily return to a previously explored thread within seven days of their first qualified encounter. Report the eligible cohort and privacy exclusions. This measures useful return behavior, not understanding, belief or self-discovery. No numerical target is justified yet; establish a baseline before setting one.

Use separately recruited, optional research sessions to assess whether people find the content worthwhile and recognise their interests in the universe. Do not insert questionnaires or tests into the normal scrolling experience. Minutes watched is a diagnostic, not the success target.

## Announcement

Open KnowScroll and find an idea worth staying with. Watch it, read it, follow it further or move somewhere new. Over time, the places you explore gather into a universe with its own character. Come back to a familiar world or visit one a friend has chosen to share. There is nothing to complete, and your worlds will still be here when you return.

## Screens — design inventory, not approved mockups

1. Encounter player: Reel and Scroll, familiar controls, vertical exploration, horizontal continuation, format switch and a clear exit.
2. Universe: formation, navigation, stable places, calm dormancy and share visibility.
3. Planet interior: regions and paths back into Reels or Scrolls.
4. Cosmic room: human discussion, separate moderator and identified AI inhabitants.
5. Privacy and history: recording controls, corrections, clearing and sharing boundaries.

No screen mockups have been created in this task. The user requested architecture documentation and no implementation; interaction decisions here require subsequent mockup and device review.
