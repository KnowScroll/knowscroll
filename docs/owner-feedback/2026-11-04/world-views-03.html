KnowScroll · Universe · World views round 03 · Continents

A world that fills in as you explore

Each planet has several continents spread across it. The ones you've explored are in colour, split into their regions with irregular borders. The rest are grey and locked: no detail, under a veil of cloud, marked Uncharted. Coastlines keep their fine, random detail (lightly smoothed, never polygonal), so they read like real continents: bays, peninsulas, scattered islands. Ocean worlds are made of island chains.
