Room-level caps (dev defaults)

AI to AI

Unprompted turns: ≤ 1 per 3 human messages, ≤ 8 per hour, ≤ 40 per day.

At most one reply between inhabitants (Null may answer Ray once). Then the next AI
turn waits for a person. No staged AI debates, ever: an argument between two bots is a
show, not a room.

Asked turns: don’t count against the unprompted cap; ≤ 10 per person per hour, ≤ 80 per room per
day.
No repeats: never two turns in a row from the same inhabitant without a person in between (a
running Launch is the exception).

Quiet-room rule

One pass, one voice: each Stage manager pass allows at most one speaking turn.
Mods can tune every number per room; the room menu shows the current limits to everyone.

An empty room costs zero live calls. The Stage manager only runs after someone posts.
What keeps a quiet room alive is made ahead in one nightly batch (Today’s question,
Spot the flaw, tomorrow’s Launch) or on return (while you were away, cached). Nothing
speaks into an empty room to keep it warm.

Topics is a Reddit-style list: title, tag (Question, Debate,
Explainer, Share), replies, and Useful instead of upvotes. No
downvotes, no karma total; Useful only feeds flair (Good
question, First answer). Sort Hot / New / Unanswered, so

WHY IT’S WORTH STAYING

MY PICK

No typing dots unless a person is typing. No bot “members” in
the member list or counts. No presence for people who aren’t
here: the pill says “Quiet right now · last message 3 h ago”. No
invented counts: “2 answered today” and “5 played last night” are
real. Replays are labelled as replays on every screen.

An empty room makes zero model calls. Today’s question and
Spot the flaw come from one nightly batch call. Replays reuse
stored answers: no calls. Ray’s warm-up offer is one unprompted
turn, once per person per day, only when a person is here. An
open question costs one asked turn; the person’s answer later
costs nothing.

There’s always something to do alone: answer today’s question,
replay last night’s Launch against real players, leave a question
that someone will answer. And everything you do here outlasts
you being here: answers stay, the sky remembers, and you’re told
when a person replies.

Lead with async play over chat. In a room of one, the Replay and
the Open questions shelf do the work that live chat does in a busy
room. The cast keeps the room company, but never pretends a
crowd is there: the honesty is what makes coming back feel
worth it.
