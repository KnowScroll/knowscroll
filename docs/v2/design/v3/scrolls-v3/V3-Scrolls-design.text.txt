You said “swipe right”. I read it as “Part 2 sits to the right”, so the finger
moves right to left, like turning a page. A left-to-right swipe from the
edge is the phone’s back gesture, and Reels already continue with ←

In a Scroll, swiping up is how you read. It can only mean “next topic” once
you’re past the end, where the pull-up meter makes it deliberate, the
same as reaching the end of a Reel. The top Next ↑ is the always-there
fallback, and N on a keyboard.
