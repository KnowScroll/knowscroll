Systems that look like systems

Round 04’s hand-drawn haze and particles are gone. The systems on the wheel and in the top bar are now drawn by the same 3D code as U08 Far and U02 System: the same sun with its bloom, the same colour haze, the same painted planets going round, no orbit lines. Two changes only: the sun’s glow is about a fifth dimmer, and the haze is a little fuller and turns a little faster.
