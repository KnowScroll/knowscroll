Your universe begins

Plays once, the first time someone opens the Universe tab. Dust streams in from off screen along five spiral arms, and the star builds up out of it from a tiny ember: a floating ball with a turning painted surface. Sparkles keep multiplying around it, it trembles, squeezes, and bursts. Then a long trip through the newborn universe, past asteroids, planets, galaxies and two systems, a slow, smooth build to light speed with stars coming straight at us, and a soft drop out of light speed into the empty universe exactly as it is finalized. Everything is drawn with the approved scene code. Skip is always there until the end.
