# Design fidelity: v3 designs against the app

The v3 designs (Universe, V3 Scrolls, R3 Rooms, T3 Talk) compared with the app on dev. This is the v3 twin of [`fidelity.md`](fidelity.md), for issue #497. It records two passes: a first sweep (**before**) and an after pass once the v3 slices and the fidelity fixes had landed (**after**).

**Dev commit.** The after pass ran on dev commit `36ca34b2` (migration `0102_room_flair`, 2 Nov 2026). The ask rows (D1) were re-run on `09adb590` (dev-check-10, 2 Nov 2026), where all five asks reached Ready.

**Method.** Phone 390 x 844 (touch) and desktop 1440 x 900, with 1280 and 1024 for the Talk columns and two short phones (390 x 664, 360 x 640) for the Universe. Disposable accounts only. No operator commands, no orders except the five asks of dev-check-10, and one model call (a Challenge in a room).

**Status words** are the v2.1 words: **matches** (same structure, copy and look at the drawn width, small spacing aside), **differs** (works, but visibly not as drawn), **missing** (no screen, or no data to draw it), **not checked** (not opened in this audit). `differs → fixed in #650, re-shot pending` means a fix has merged but nobody has looked at it on dev yet; it counts as differs until it is re-shot. A status that moved since the first sweep reads `status (was old status)`.

**Every differs or missing row has a reason:** the decision that accepts it, an owner call, or the merged PR that supersedes the shot.

**Screenshots live on the coordinator's machine, not in the repository.** The Before and After cells are paths under `C:/Users/XZNON/knowscroll-dev/tmp/fidelity-v3/`. Each pair has the design on the left and the app on the right; rows with no design frame show the app alone; animations are frame strips with the time on each frame. A Before of `none` means the first sweep had no shot for that row.
