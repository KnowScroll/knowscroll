U08

Mature universe

A year in: a dozen systems across several families. Labels follow zoom levels, quiet areas merge into named sky regions, and search or the outline jump straight to any place. Nothing is counted.

Prototype · design data

Label levels (LOD)

Far: system names only; quiet systems close together merge into a named sky region (“Arts & ideas”). Mid: planets and rooms of systems in view; lower-priority labels fade to dots. Near: regions. Priority = how recently you visited, then size on screen. Labels never overlap; crowded ones get leader lines.

Actions

Primary: tap a system/cluster to select (yellow ring + compact card), second tap or Open to enter. Secondary: search, Lately lens, list view, pinch/scroll zoom, drag to pan. Tapping a merged region zooms in until its systems separate.

States & overlays

far · mid · near · selected · search (typing, results, no match: “Nothing here called that yet”) · list view · low detail (list + flat tiles) when the renderer falls back or the map is very large · stale scene refreshing quietly.

Commands · observations

OBS universe.zoom{level} · universe.select{id} · universe.search{resultKind} · universe.return
Search runs on your own map index only; queries are not logged with their text.

Restore

Camera {center, zoom, selection} is a history entry: Back after a search or fly-to returns to the previous camera. Leaving the tab and returning keeps the last camera; a long absence starts framed on everything with the ship at your last place.

Accessibility & platform

List view is the complete equivalent (headings per family, links per system, planets nested). Merged regions announce their members. Search is a real input with a listbox. Web: / focuses search, +/− zoom, arrows pan. Label budget per viewport keeps rendering cheap on low-end phones.
