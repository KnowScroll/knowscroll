C03

Thread invitation & membership

You can look before you join. Joining is one clear tap on Join thread; Ignore hides it quietly. What’s shared is stated once, plainly.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

Created by H01 “Explore with friends” or C02 “Start a thread”. Recipients see a card in Talk and an optional notification. Preview opens /thread/:threadId?preview read-only (no messages sent, no presence shown); Join lands them at Part 1 as a member.

Actions

Primary: Join thread. Secondary: Preview, Ignore, and after joining Mute, Leave, Add people (respects blocks), own status (F02). Product decision (v2.1): joining is one tap on Join; preview first, no separate accept/decline ceremony; Ignore hides it without telling Asha.

States & overlays

new · previewing (not a member) · joined · ignored (hidden, can still be found under Hidden) · muted · left · removed · ended by starter · item withdrawn/corrected · blocked person can’t be added (no hint why). People who hide status show no label.

Commands · observations

CMD thread.join · thread.ignore · thread.mute · thread.leave · thread.add_member{handle} · thread.end · thread.remove_member
Presence: coarse state honouring each member’s setting.
Membership never merges universes or shares positions.

Restore

Re-opening returns each person to their own item, playhead and conversation scroll. After ending or removal, a stale link lands on the thread with the ended line — never an error page.

Accessibility & platform

The new-thread card is a labelled region with real buttons. Presence is text, not just a dot. Leave is a destructive action with its own confirmation sheet. Native: notification opens the thread; web: the same link.
