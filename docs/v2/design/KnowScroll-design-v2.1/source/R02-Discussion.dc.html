R02

Room threads & explorations

Same model as private threads, different membership. Each exploration belongs to one room thread, so groups can follow different what-ifs without changing the whole room’s topic. AI may suggest; people decide what gets made.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

/room/:roomId lists room threads; /room/:roomId/t/:threadId opens one. Reached from the station on your map (R01), Talk, or a notification. Same thread component as private threads (C04).

Actions

Primary: share into the room, discuss, share a moment, ask What if… Secondary: Explore / Dismiss an AI suggestion, invite an AI inhabitant, report. Moderator: pin, move to own thread, mark helpful, close to new branches.

States & overlays

room ready · thread empty · AI suggestion pending human choice · dismissed · exploration preparing/ready · send failed → retry same bubble · removed by moderator · reported · access lost · quiet room (honest, no filler).

Commands · observations

CMD room.thread.create{roomId, assetRevision}
CMD branch.create{threadId, source: ai_suggestion|message|snippet}
CMD room.pin{threadId|branchId} · moderation.remove · report.abuse
AI suggestions are messages, never commands.

Restore

Each room thread keeps your own playback position and branch node. Leaving to the room list and back restores scroll. Room messages are conversation, not encounter evidence; joining never merges universes.

Accessibility & platform

AI messages carry the spoken label “AI, Skeptic” before content; dotted outline plus square corner, never colour alone. Moderator actions are a labelled dialog. Desktop: room info left, thread centre, optional activity right (D04).
