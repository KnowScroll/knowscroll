E02

Scroll article

“Attention, slowly”: the Scroll form of the Part 1 explanation. Vertical movement reads first. The end of the article is a boundary, not a launch pad.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

/s/:id#block from the Reel switch (E06), Keep resume, a share card or a region. Opens a specific article revision; the anchor is the block ID, not a pixel offset.

Actions

Primary: read. Secondary: the same Like/Save/Share/Ask strip as the Reel rail, Reel ⇄ Scroll. At the end: Part 2 (solid yellow), Related (dashed), Next subject. Nav hides while reading and returns at the end.

States & overlays

loading skeleton · reading · block interacting · end boundary · block render fallback · partial download/offline · corrected (banner under title) · unavailable (E01 pattern). Gesture: vertical reads first; a block owns its drag until release; past the boundary you need a fresh swipe or a button.

Commands · observations

CMD encounter.save · thread.continue{series|related} · subject.next · format.switch
OBS scroll.block_coverage · scroll.active_dwell · scroll.block_interaction{blockId}

Restore

Anchor = article revision + block ID + offset within the block, plus each block’s own state. Returning from Share, Ask or a Part restores the exact anchor and flashes the paragraph (content.highlight). A new revision maps the anchor or falls back to the section heading.

Accessibility & platform

Headings are real h2/h3 for rotor and landmark navigation. Web: text selection allowed; Space/PgDn scroll; boundary keys never trigger the next subject. Native: the back edge returns to the Reel. Reading line is decorative; position is announced by section.
