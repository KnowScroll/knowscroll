↑ ↓ subjectSwipe or arrow keys move through the feed. Next and previous subject.
← → threadMove within the current idea. Every arrow points to its destination: next is on the right.
Back = your historySystem back, browser Back and the top-bar chevron retrace your own path one step, restoring position.
