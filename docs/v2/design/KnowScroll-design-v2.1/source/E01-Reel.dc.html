E01

Dimensional TV · Reel

Full-bleed encounter. One action rail shared with Scroll; one Reel ⇄ Scroll switch with equal weight. Swipe up changes subject; swipe left or the right-edge tab (→ points to the destination) continues the idea.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

/explore tab root · deep link /e/:encounterId?t=31 from Keep, share card, region. Opens the exact asset revision; a corrected revision shows the banner once per person.

Actions

Primary: watch · swipe up / ↓ key = next subject · swipe left / → key / right-edge tab = continue in thread. Back retraces your own history (N01). Secondary: Like, Save, Share, Ask, Reel ⇄ Scroll, captions. Rail order is identical on Scroll (E02).

States & overlays

playing · paused · buffering · offline · ended → E05/E08 · corrected · unavailable · prefetched-not-visible (emits nothing). Overlays: share H01, ask E04, feedback P03 (long-press / ⋯).

Commands · observations

CMD encounter.like · encounter.save · subject.next · thread.continue{series} · format.switch
OBS reel.visible_interval · reel.pause · reel.buffer · app.visibility

Restore

Playhead per exposure (0:31), captions preference and thread position. Closing a sheet (Share, Ask, menu) resumes playback — you never left. Returning via Back from a Scroll or another screen resumes paused at the same second — never autoplay with sound after a detour.

Accessibility & platform

Rail buttons announce state (“Save, not saved”). Swipe equivalents: ↑/↓ and ←/→ keys (web), Next subject in ⋯ menu, edge tab is a real link. Captions default on when system captions are on. Back (native back edge/button, browser Back) is one rule everywhere: one step back through your own history; sheets close first.
