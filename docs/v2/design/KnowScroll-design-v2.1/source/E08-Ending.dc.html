E08

Natural thread ending

A satisfying stop is a designed state, not an error. Keep a way back, pick something different, or simply be done — no guilt, no streak.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

Shown in place of the next buffer slot when a declared series and its related moves are exhausted, or after an explicit “Done”. Same route as the thread: /e/:id (end state).

Actions

Primary: Keep a way back. Secondary: Something different, Done for now (returns to a calm Explore with no autoplay), Undo, See it in Keep, Ask for something.

States & overlays

ending card · way back kept · choose different · way back failed (save banner with Retry, card stays). Titles only — no counts, minutes, “you learned” or progress meters.

Commands · observations

CMD keep.return_path{threadId, anchor}
CMD subject.next{origin: ending}
OBS thread.ended · ending.shown

Restore

Return path opens the last step at its end anchor with the thread trail intact. Leaving and returning to Explore later starts fresh; the ending card is not re-shown unless the thread is reopened.

Accessibility & platform

Card heading receives focus on arrival; buttons in reading order. Reduced motion: card fades in, no slide. Native back from the ending returns to Part 3 paused at its end. Web: Escape = Done for now.
