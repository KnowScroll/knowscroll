v2

What changed in v2

From the review

Five fixes

  • Individual worlds — the same ML topic for Asha, Ravi and Meera, with different coastlines, terrain, light, landmarks, routes and neighbours (U07); a mature universe with many systems, label levels, search and list (U08); the planet close-up redrawn (U03).
  • Content range — a history Reel with imagery and narration, an editorial Scroll, a football Reel with movement and captions, an interactive-comparison Scroll (E09).
  • One navigation rule — arrows always point to the destination; Back retraces your own path; overlays close first. Worked example with the stack at every step (N01, Journey 5).
  • No engineering copy in the app — “owns your drag”, “momentum stops here”, “no follow-up questions” replaced by natural invitations (E03, E02, E04).
  • Privacy matches the architecture — one “Clear exploration” operation, no time-range clearing, accurate message wording; friend-visit fog is generic decoration (P02, U06).
New sharing model

“Here’s something interesting. Come explore it with me.”

  • Share offers Send to a conversation · Explore with friends · Share a moment · Copy link (H01).
  • Content threads hold the sequence and the conversation together: navigator with your own ✓/current/upcoming/Preparing, collapsible player, chat; works when people arrive at different times (C04, C03).
  • Snippets make talk specific: a Reel range, a Scroll passage, or an interactive block with its settings (H03).
  • What if… grows out of the conversation: from the thread, a snippet or a message; “What if…” vs “Another perspective”; branch cards; speculative ideas labelled as such; optional branch map (X01–X04).
  • Rooms use the same model: explorations live in room threads; moderators pin; AI suggests but people choose (R02). Presence (“Online”, “Recently active”) is optional and never shows what someone watches.
v2.1 refinements · decisions recorded
  • Episode browser: thumbnail cards with part, short title and your own marker; compact numbers only when the player is collapsed; Preparing kept apart and not selectable (C04, D03).
  • Decision · collapse pauses. Collapsing the thread player pauses and keeps the playhead. Listening while chatting is an explicit audio-only choice, never counted as viewing. A visible action strip (Like · Save · Moment · Full screen) and a keyboard-open frame are drawn (C04).
  • Decision · quick what-if. A complete request submits directly with a concise editable summary; only speculative or ambiguous ideas get the assumption step (X01, Journey 2).
  • Honest promises: privacy receipts separate “cleared from active systems” (completes) from backup expiry (pending, within [N days]); corrected snippets play a mapped moment only when a compatible mapping exists (P02, H03).
  • Decision · joining a thread. Preview first; joining is one explicit tap on “Join thread”; “Ignore” hides it without telling the sender. No separate accept/decline ceremony (C03).
  • Desktop room now uses room threads: room/thread navigation, the selected thread’s encounter and inline branches; the poll moves to a collapsible side panel (D04).
01

Reference audit

Preserve

What already works

  • Poster Scroll (01, 06): cream paper, 2px ink outlines, cobalt offset shadow on the hero card, heavy Bricolage headlines.
  • Yellow "Continue this idea" rail (01): promoted to the single cue for horizontal continuation.
  • Spacious universe (02, 03): one star per system, hairline orbits, lots of negative space.
  • Teal globe + angular land + small ship (04, 05): the ship becomes the "you are here / exact return" marker.
  • Cream floating pills as the only controls in space.
Refine

Same identity, better behaved

  • One space ground: #050B19 / #03101A → space.0 #040A17; #0B1630 / #061A27 → space.1 #0B1A2E.
  • Reading: 18/28 ink body, ~62ch measure; DM Mono only for labels.
  • Reel ⇄ Scroll: one equal segmented switch, same place on both formats, "same explanation" glyph.
  • Labels in space: fixed baseline under each body, leader lines, max two lines, never over another body.
  • Nav pill: kept dark with cobalt active; renamed Explore · Universe · Talk · Keep.
Replace

Deliberately removed

  • Grey Worlds dialog (08) → selection happens in the map; the list alternative is a cream sheet with hierarchy.
  • Overlapping repeated globes (07) → each planet appears once; labels never collide.
  • Provenance/debug copy (06, 04): "Documented", "Why this appeared", fixture notes, "2 sources", "SEEN" — gone from consumer UI.
  • Counts (02, 05): "3 stops", "2 free planets" → no quantities that invite comparison.
  • Moons (05) → removed so nothing implies meaning.

Only two epistemic labels survive in ordinary screens — Hypothetical (what-if content) and Corrected (a revised asset) — because they change what the reader should believe. Everything else about provenance stays internal.

04

Versus the current theme

Same two registers, same palette and fonts. The change is discipline: tokens are semantic (content vs spatial roles), not raw hexes per screen.

Content gains a real reading rhythm, one action rail shared by Reel and Scroll, and four distinct relationship cues (Part, Related, Format, What-if) instead of one generic "next".

Space keeps the calm composition but drops counts, moons, duplicate globes and metadata-first sheets. Topic families share a hue and silhouette; each person's terrain, orbit order and routes vary.

Navigation moves from Cable / Atlas / Keep / Ask to Explore / Universe / Talk / Keep. Ask lives on the encounter itself.

05

Open design decisions

  1. Renderer: drawn here as layered 2D (SVG/canvas-feasible). 3D stays optional; semantics don't depend on it.
  2. Tab labels: "Talk" vs "Conversations"; "Keep" vs "Saved". Four tabs vs three plus a header button.
  3. Guest browsing: A01 shows signed-out preview of shared content only; persisted guest identity not assumed.
  4. Auth method: email link drawn as a placeholder; no provider commitment.
  5. Room placement: station between two systems vs on the orbit of the stronger one.
  6. Lately lens: whether recent-activity glow is on by default when returning after absence.
  7. Desktop split: map/conversation beside the encounter — confirm single active playback rule in user testing.
  8. Family hues: five starter families drawn; how new families get hues needs a rule.
  9. Snippet settings: whether an interactive-block snippet includes its configuration by default (drawn as an opt-in toggle).
  10. Presence default: “Recently active” vs off for new accounts; online status is drawn as opt-in.
  11. Back at a tab root: platform convention vs returning to the previous tab — needs device testing.
