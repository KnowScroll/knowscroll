A01–A03

Entry, recovery, first arrival

Easy entry with no interest survey. Recovery always promises to bring you back where you were. First arrival goes straight to a Reel with one dismissible hint.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

/welcome → /signin → /signin/link; expired session or bad link → /recover. A deep link (share, notification) is held as the destination through the whole flow. Guest browsing is not assumed.

Actions

Primary: Continue with email · Sign in again · Send a new link. Secondary: I have a link, change email, Got it. Auth method stays open: the placeholder row reserves space without committing to a provider.

States & overlays

signed out · link requested · link sent · redeeming · expired session · link expired/used · offline (send disabled with banner) · first arrival hint (shown once, dismissible). No questionnaire or interest picker at any step.

Commands · observations

CMD auth.request_link{email} · auth.redeem_link{token}
OBS onboarding.hint_dismissed · app.first_open

Restore

After sign-in, open the held destination exactly (the Scroll at “Why squared?”). With no destination, first arrival opens Explore on a ready Reel from a non-personal starter set. The universe starts empty.

Accessibility & platform

Focus order: wordmark → primary → secondary. Email field has a label and autofill (web autocomplete=email). Native opens the magic link via universal/app links; web redeems in the same tab. The hint is a real note with a focusable dismiss button.
