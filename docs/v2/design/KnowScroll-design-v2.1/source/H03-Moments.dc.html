H03

Share a moment · snippets

“Look at this” becomes an exact place everyone can open: a short range of a Reel, a passage of a Scroll, or an interactive block set the way you set it. Snippets point at the original — no new video is made.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

Share → Share a moment (H01), long-press on a Reel’s progress bar, text selection in a Scroll, or the share control on an interactive block. Opening a snippet: /thread/:id/snippet/:snippetId, a focused viewer over the thread.

Actions

Primary: choose a second or a short range / a passage / a block state, add a message, share. Secondary: include or leave out block settings; Replay; Watch from here (continues the full Reel at 0:18 as your own encounter).

States & overlays

picking · sending · sent · failed → retry same request · original corrected: compatible mapping exists → card shows the mapped range and Play opens it; no compatible mapping → card says the moment changed, offers the revised Reel with one line on what changed, and never plays the old range · original withdrawn (card stays, content “unavailable”) · block type changed (falls back to default settings, says so).

Commands · observations

CMD snippet.share{threadId, assetRevision, start, end}
CMD snippet.share{…, blockId, blockState}
OBS snippet.opened · reel.visible_interval (range only)
No new media file; the card is a reference + position.

Restore

The viewer is not a separate history entry for the thread: Back (or “Back to thread”) closes it and restores the conversation scroll position and your own place in the navigator. “Watch from here” pushes the full Reel as a normal history entry.

Accessibility & platform

Range handles are real controls: ←/→ nudge by a second, Shift+←/→ by five; screen readers announce “Start 0:18, end 0:31”. Passage sharing uses native text selection (web context menu, iOS/Android selection bar).
