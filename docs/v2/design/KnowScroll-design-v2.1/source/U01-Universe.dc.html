U01

Universe overview

A calm, spacious map of what you have actually explored. Systems are broad topics, stars organise them; nothing is counted, scored or lost after time away.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

/universe tab root. Optional ?focus=last after absence puts the ship at the last place. Opens from Explore breadcrumb, Keep item “Open in Universe”, or a region link.

Actions

Primary: tap a system to fly in (U02). Secondary: Lately lens (recent activity glow on/off), List view (accessible outline), room station → R01, Return to last place. Pinch/scroll zooms; drag pans.

States & overlays

empty · emerging (dust/protostar, unlabelled) · established · returning (calm, intact) · stale scene refresh (old scene stays, quietly redraws) · low-detail fallback (U03-3). Dust note is a small cream popover, never a dialog.

Commands · observations

OBS universe.open · universe.select{system} · universe.lens{lately} · universe.return
No CMD here except navigation. Map visits are never content evidence and never grow a world.

Restore

Camera {center, zoom} and Lately lens persist per device. After absence the camera starts framed on everything, ship at last region visited. Formation transitions not yet seen play once, then show final state (U05).

Accessibility & platform

Each body has a 48px hit button with name + kind (“Machine learning, a system”). Tab order: title, Lately, systems by reading order, List view, nav. Reduced motion stops twinkle and drift. Web: arrow keys pan, +/− zoom; native: pinch.
