X01

What if… from the conversation

A clear request goes straight through with a short summary you can edit. Only ambiguous or speculative ideas ask you to confirm assumptions first — casual talk shouldn’t feel like configuring a simulation.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

Opened inside a content thread /thread/:threadId or a room thread. Three entry points: the thread’s What if… button, the button on a snippet card, or a message’s ⋯ menu. The source travels as “From: …” and can be changed in the sheet.

Actions

Rule: a complete request (one clear change + what stays) submits directly from the sheet with an editable one-line summary. Ambiguous, contradictory or speculative ideas go to the confirm step first. Assumptions stay editable on the branch card until content is made.

States & overlays

composer empty · typing · summary forming (shown once the idea is clear) · clear → Explore · unclear → one question or confirm step · speculative label required · branch preparing / ready / failed (X02) · offline (draft kept, Explore disabled with reason).

Commands · observations

CMD scenario.ask{threadId, text, assumptions, direct: true}
CMD scenario.clarify{answer} · branch.propose{kind: what_if|perspective}
CMD branch.edit_assumptions · branch.cancel
OBS whatif.composer_opened{source}

Restore

Closing the sheet returns to the same conversation scroll; the collapsed player stays paused where it was. A draft survives leaving the thread. The sheet is an overlay, not a Back entry.

Accessibility & platform

The summary is read as “Changed: traffic times ten. Kept: same model.” Edit is a real button. Speculative status is text, not colour. Web: Enter submits when the summary is shown, Esc closes. Native: message menu from a visible ⋯ as well as long-press.
