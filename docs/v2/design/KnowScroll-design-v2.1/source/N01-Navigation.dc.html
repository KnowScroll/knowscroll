Navigation & Back — one worked example

Asha watches Part 1 → opens its Scroll → continues to Part 2 → opens Share → closes it → presses Back. Three rules: vertical = subject, horizontal = thread, Back = your own history. Arrows always point to where you’ll go.

Needs a touch prototype on real devices • End of a Scroll: does a fast flick settle calmly on the end mark, and does a fresh swipe feel deliberate, not sticky? • Blocks inside a Scroll: dragging the diagram or slider vs scrolling the page — no accidental page moves, no trapped scrolling. • Edges: Android/iOS back-edge vs in-app horizontal swipe (24px inset), landscape and larger text. • Closing a sheet resumes playback without a jump in audio.

Route & origin

Applies to every tab. Each history entry is a route plus a position: /e/:id?t=31, /s/:id#block, /x/:id/:node, /universe/…?cam=.

Actions

Three axes only: vertical = subject, horizontal = thread, Back = your history. Arrows on tabs and buttons point to the destination, never to the swipe direction.

States & overlays

Overlays (Share, Ask, menus, trail sheet) never enter history. Deep links start a fresh stack with Explore root beneath them.

Commands · observations

CMD subject.next / previous · thread.continue / previous · format.switch
Back is client navigation: no command, no evidence.

Restore

Reel: paused at playhead after a real detour; plays on after an overlay. Scroll: anchor block + offset. Branch: node. Map: camera. Stacks survive app backgrounding.

Accessibility & platform

Every swipe has a key and a visible control. Back chevron is a real button named “Back to …”. Web history mirrors the stack; native uses the platform back.
