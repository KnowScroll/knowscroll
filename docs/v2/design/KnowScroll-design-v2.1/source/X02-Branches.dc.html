X02

Branch cards in the conversation

A chosen idea becomes a card in the same chat. What-if cards are dashed cobalt with a branch mark; perspective cards are solid with their own title. Neither is numbered as a part.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

Cards live in the thread timeline /t/:threadId. “Explore this” opens /t/:threadId/b/:branchId (X03). Same card component inside room threads (R02).

Actions

Primary: Explore this. Secondary: reply to the card, Try again (same request), Remove (creator), make a dismissed suggestion. Anyone can open; nobody is moved when someone else does.

States & overlays

proposed · preparing (explicit, no %) · ready · failed (retry/remove) · removed · suggestion not made · speculative label · source content withdrawn (card stays, says the root is unavailable).

Commands · observations

CMD branch.create{threadId, kind, assumptions}
CMD branch.retry{sameRequestId} · branch.remove
CMD message.reply{target: branchId}
Receipt branch.ready → card update via socket

Restore

Card status updates in place; it never jumps the chat or steals focus. Returning from a branch lands on the card you opened it from, with the conversation scroll intact.

Accessibility & platform

Each card is an article with a labelled heading; kind and speculative status are spoken words (“What if, speculative”). Preparing is announced politely once. Native and web share the component; desktop shows it in the conversation column (D03).
