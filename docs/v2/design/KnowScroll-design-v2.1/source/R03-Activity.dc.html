R03

Optional room activity

Polls and quizzes exist only inside a room you chose to enter. Never in the feed, never required, never scored, never part of how worlds form.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

/room/:roomId/activity/:activityId — only from inside a room (R02 header “Poll”, a moderator post). Never linked from Explore, notifications about streaks, or the universe.

Actions

Primary: vote / answer. Secondary: skip, change vote before close, skip question, leave quiz, discuss this. Every exit is equal weight — no “are you sure?”.

States & overlays

open · voted (change allowed) · closed · quiz in progress · answers view · activity removed by moderator · offline (vote queued, shown as “Not sent yet”). No score, grade, rank, streak or leaderboard.

Commands · observations

CMD room.poll.vote{activityId, optionId}
CMD room.quiz.answer{activityId, questionId, optionId}
OBS room.activity_opened / skipped
None of these are encounter evidence or inputs to formation.

Restore

Quiz position is kept for the session only; leaving discards it, with no “unfinished” nag. A poll remembers your vote until it closes.

Accessibility & platform

Options are a radiogroup (arrow keys, Space to choose) with selection shown by fill, a dot and an offset shadow — not colour alone. Question counter is text. On desktop the activity sits in the D04 side panel beside the discussion.
