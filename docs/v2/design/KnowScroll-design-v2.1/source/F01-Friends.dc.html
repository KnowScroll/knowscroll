F01–F02

Friends & sharing settings

Friends come from handles and invite links you choose — never an address-book upload. Visibility is per system, and revocation is spelled out honestly.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

/friends, /friends/add, /settings/visibility. Reached from Account (P01), the share sheet’s “Add a friend”, and an invite link landing (/share/:token → request).

Actions

Primary: Add a friend, Accept. Secondary: copy invite link, decline, block/unblock, turn a link off, choose visitors, share or hide each system, preview as a friend.

States & overlays

empty (no friends: invite card) · pending sent · incoming request · accepted · declined (silent to sender) · blocked · link revoked · visibility saving / saved / failed-retry. Hidden systems are simply absent for visitors — any haze is generic decoration, never sized or placed to match them. Activity status: Online · Recently active only · Don’t show.

Commands · observations

CMD friend.invite{handle|link} · friend.accept · friend.decline · friend.block · friend.unblock · invite_link.revoke · visibility.set{scope, systemId?} · presence.set{online|recent|hidden}
OBS invite_link.copied
Presence never carries what someone is watching.

Restore

Revoking or narrowing visibility applies to every future read immediately. We say plainly: screenshots and anything already seen can’t be clawed back. Scroll position in long lists restores on back.

Accessibility & platform

Switches use role=switch with the system name; radio rows are a labelled fieldset. Native: “More apps…” opens the OS share sheet; web: Web Share where available, else copy. Handle field is keyboard-submit.
