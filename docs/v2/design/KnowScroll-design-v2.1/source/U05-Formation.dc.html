U05

Formation storyboard

A 2.4-second sequence that plays once, only after a change has actually been applied. It shows a new place; it never says what you learned. Skip is always available; reduced motion shows the last frame.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

Plays on /universe or /universe/:system the next time the person opens the map after a committed geography change. Never interrupts a Reel or Scroll.

Actions

Primary: watch (2.4s) or Skip on any frame → final state. After: Done or tap the planet → U03. No share prompt, no celebration, no “you learned”.

States & overlays

Dust · gathering · protostar · star & orbit (new system only) · planet settles. A planet in an existing system skips beats 3–4. Reconnect / late open / already seen → final state directly.

Commands · observations

OBS formation.shown{transitionId} · formation.skipped{transitionId}
Driven by a server delta id; the client never decides that something formed. A stale delta after a reset is never replayed.

Restore

Played transition IDs stored per account so the sequence shows once across devices. After it ends the camera rests on the new place; leaving mid-sequence counts as skipped.

Accessibility & platform

Status text “A new place: Attention” announced politely once. Skip is the first focus stop. Reduced motion (system or P01): still + caption only. No flashes; glow changes stay under 3 per second.
