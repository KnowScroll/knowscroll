C04

Content thread

The sequence and the conversation on one screen. An episode browser with real thumbnails and short titles shows your own place; collapsing the player pauses it; Like, Save, Share moment and Full screen are always one tap away. Works when everyone arrives at different times.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

/thread/:threadId from H01 “Explore with friends”, C02 “Start a thread”, the inbox or a notification; item deep link /thread/:id/item/:itemId?t=23. A thread can start from one Reel or Scroll: then the browser shows one card plus “Continue this”, which becomes a Preparing card when requested. Desktop puts content beside the conversation (D03).

Actions

Primary: watch/read the selected item, talk. Always visible: Like · Save · Share moment · Full screen, and ⋯ (Keep listening, Report). Browser: horizontally scrollable cards (thumbnail · part · short title · your marker); the last card is cut off to show it scrolls; “See all” opens the grid. Collapsed: numbered chips + mini bar (Play/Pause · Expand · ⋯).

States & overlays

Rule: collapsing pauses. Keyboard opening collapses (and so pauses). Keep listening is an explicit ⋯ choice: audio only, video hidden, not counted as watching. Card states: watched ✓ · Now · no mark · Preparing (separate, dashed, not selectable) · perspective / what-if items with their own titles, never numbered. Loading, offline (cached), withdrawn/corrected items as in C03/H03.

Commands · observations

CMD thread.select_item{itemId} · thread.continue{fromItem} · message.send{requestId}
CMD thread.player.collapse → pause (playhead kept)
CMD thread.player.keep_listening{on}
OBS reel.visible_interval stops on collapse/keyboard; audio-only time is not reel.visible_interval
Others’ positions are never sent; ✓ is your own history.

Restore

Per person: selected item, playhead (kept when collapsed, e.g. 0:23), reading anchor, expanded/collapsed state, browser scroll and conversation scroll. Closing the keyboard does not auto-resume video; Play is one tap on the mini bar. Back from full screen returns to the expanded thread.

Accessibility & platform

Cards are buttons named like “Part 2, The edge, now playing”; markers are words + glyphs, not colour. The browser is a labelled group; arrow keys move between cards (web), swipe scrolls (native). Keyboard frame: composer stays above the system keyboard, the last 3–4 messages and a snippet stay readable, Play stays reachable. No fake keyboard is drawn.
