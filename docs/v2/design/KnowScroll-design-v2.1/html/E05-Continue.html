E05

Horizontal continuation

Horizontal moves follow the current idea: swipe left, or tap the right-edge tab (→ points to where you’ll go). A declared sequel (solid yellow, “Part N”) and a related angle (dashed cream, “Related”) never look alike. The trail remembers every step’s exact place.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

/e/:id?thread=:threadId — any Reel or Scroll with a declared next part or related item. Trail sheet is an overlay on the current encounter; no route change.

Actions

Primary: swipe left, → key or the right-edge tab = next step (Part or Related). Secondary: swipe right, ← key or the left-edge tab = previous step in this thread; trail jump; Ask for more; Next subject. Arrows point to the destination. Back is separate: it retraces your own history (N01).

States & overlays

series step · related step · trail sheet · requested missing part (→ E07) · exhausted · corrected prior. Never auto-advance at the end of a Reel; the tab just waits.

Commands · observations

CMD thread.continue{relation: series|related, from, to}
CMD thread.previous{to} · thread.jump{stepId}
OBS thread.trail_opened · reel.visible_interval

Restore

Each visited step keeps its own anchor: playhead for Reels, block anchor for Scrolls. Returning to a step resumes paused at that anchor; the trail highlights where you were.

Accessibility & platform

Edge tabs are links named “Continue: Part 3, …” / “Another related explanation”. Web: ← / → keys move within the thread; browser Back retraces history. Native: the system back edge is always Back (one history step, N01), never “previous part”; the in-app horizontal swipe starts 24px in from the edge. Trail rows are a list with aria-current.
