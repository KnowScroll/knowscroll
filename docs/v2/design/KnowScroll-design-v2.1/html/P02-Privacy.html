P02

Privacy & history

Every control says exactly what it changes. One clearing operation — Clear exploration — and no time-range removal. Pausing, export, clearing and deletion produce receipts that stay pending until the work is really done — and say so when it fails.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

/settings/privacy, /settings/privacy/export, /settings/privacy/clear, /settings/account/delete. From Account (P01), the paused-recording banner, and receipt notifications.

Actions

Primary: toggle recording, request export, confirm Clear exploration or delete. Secondary: download, retry a failed receipt. One clearing operation only — no time ranges; destructive steps need an explicit acknowledgement.

States & overlays

recording on/paused · export pending/ready/expired/failed · clear confirm → clearing → cleared from live systems (completes) + backup expiry within [N days] (separate pending line, never blocks “cleared”) / failed · delete pending/failed. Offline: controls read-only with an explanation; nothing queues silently.

Commands · observations

CMD privacy.recording.set{on|off} · privacy.export.request · privacy.clear_exploration · account.delete · receipt.retry{receiptId}
No OBS emitted on these screens beyond navigation.

Restore

Clear exploration and delete advance the privacy epoch: history, derived personal state and geography go; old work and late model results can’t re-apply. Keep items, conversation membership and messages are untouched by clearing. Caches, recorder buffers and diagnostics for the scope are purged.

Accessibility & platform

Status = glyph + word + colour (Preparing / Ready / Failed). Danger button stays disabled until the checkbox is ticked; focus moves to the receipt after submit. Native: export opens the share/save sheet; web: file download.
