Feedback & report

Preferences take one tap and need no reason. Reports are scoped, anchored to the moment, and acknowledged with a receipt.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

Overlay, no route of its own: opened from ⋯ or long-press on Reel/Scroll (E01/E02), a message’s menu (C02, R02), or a friend’s universe (U06). Content error deep links carry ?t=31 / block ID.

Actions

Primary: Not interested (one tap, instant). Secondary: hide subject for a while, report error, report abuse, copy link, block. Reasons are optional everywhere except picking an abuse category.

States & overlays

menu · acknowledged + Undo (timed) · error form → sending → received / failed-retry (same request ID) · abuse form → received receipt · offline: report queued with visible “Will send when online”.

Commands · observations

CMD feedback.not_interested{scope: item} · feedback.hide_subject{subjectId, duration} · feedback.undo · report.content_error{revisionId, anchor} · report.abuse{target, category} · friend.block
OBS menu.opened

Restore

Not interested advances to the next subject but keeps the Reel’s place if Undo is tapped. Hide-subject affects the feed only — existing worlds stay as they are. Closing a form returns to the same playhead, paused.

Accessibility & platform

Long-press always has the visible ⋯ equivalent. Menu items are buttons with two-line labels; chips expose pressed state. Ack banner is announced politely and keeps Undo focusable. Web: menu opens on right-click and Shift+F10.
