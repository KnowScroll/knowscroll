U03

Planet close-up

Asha’s Inference & serving, drawn as a place: fjord coastline, terraced plateaus, daylight terminator, a coastal route and two landmarks. Regions are pins on its land, not rows in a sheet.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

/universe/ml/inference from a planet tap in U02, list view, search (U08) or an encounter breadcrumb. Arrival dollies the camera and flies the ship in from the system orbit.

Actions

Primary: tap a region pin → focus, second tap or card → U04. Secondary: back pill to the system; pinch to rotate/zoom; low-detail toggle in P01 Accessibility.

States & overlays

arrival · region focus · low detail (also the automatic renderer fallback; keeps the same silhouette so it still feels like Asha’s world) · pending scene chunk (dashed pin, “Drawing…”) · offline (last scene cached).

Commands · observations

OBS universe.select{planet} · universe.select{region} · universe.render_fallback{reason}
Terraces, clouds and landmarks come from a stable seed; they never imply more evidence. Pins map 1:1 to supported regions.

Restore

Camera {planet, rotation, zoom, selectedRegion} is a history entry. Leaving for an encounter and pressing Back lands on the same rotation with the same pin selected (U04-3).

Accessibility & platform

Every pin has a 48px link named “Name, a region”. Labels sit on pills so contrast holds over land and sea. Reduced motion: no dolly, no cloud drift. Web: arrow keys rotate, Enter opens; native: drag rotates, back edge returns to the system.
