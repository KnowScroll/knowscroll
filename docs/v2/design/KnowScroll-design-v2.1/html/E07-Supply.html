E07

Supply waiting & availability

Quiet status attached to what you asked for. Five honest states — requested, preparing, ready, couldn’t prepare, cancelled — and a plain “that’s what’s ready for now”. No percentages, no ETAs.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

Request chip appears where the request was made (E04, E06). Sheet: /keep?view=requested or tap a chip. Exhaustion card replaces the next buffer slot in /explore.

Actions

Primary: Open ready item · Try again. Secondary: Cancel, Ask differently, Another subject, Something you kept. Retry and Cancel act on the request, not on the feed.

States & overlays

requested · preparing · ready · couldn’t prepare · cancelled · unavailable (“can’t be made”) · exhausted. Offline: chips keep their last known state, marked “may be out of date”.

Commands · observations

CMD request.retry{sameRequestId}
CMD request.cancel{requestId} · request.open
OBS requests.sheet_opened · feed.exhausted_shown

Restore

Opening a ready item from the sheet returns to the sheet’s scroll position; the encounter you left stays paused at its anchor. Ready state persists until opened or dismissed — notifications only if enabled in P01.

Accessibility & platform

Chip state is word + glyph, never colour alone. Status changes announce politely. Native: optional push “Ready” only. Web: same in-app toast; no browser push by default.
