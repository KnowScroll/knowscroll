Account & settings

Reached from your avatar. Sessions, comfort settings and notifications — no streaks, no “you missed” nudges.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

/settings, /settings/accessibility, /settings/notifications. Opened from the avatar on Keep and Universe headers; deep-linked from a notification’s “Manage”.

Actions

Primary: change a setting (applies immediately). Secondary: edit profile, sign out a session, sign out here, jump to Friends or Privacy (P02).

States & overlays

loading (rows skeleton) · saving (switch disabled, spinner glyph) · saved · failed (switch reverts + banner “Couldn’t save · Retry”) · offline (switches read-only, explained). Signing out a session asks once.

Commands · observations

CMD settings.set{key, value} · session.revoke{sessionId} · auth.sign_out · notifications.set{channel} · quiet_hours.set

Restore

Settings sync per account across devices. Reduce motion and text size mirror the OS until changed here. Returning to a tab restores its own place; settings never reset reading or playback positions.

Accessibility & platform

Every switch has role=switch and its row label. Text-size preview is live. Native: notification permission prompt only after a switch is turned on; web: browser permission, with a plain fallback if denied.
