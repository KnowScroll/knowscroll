U02

Topic system

Machine learning, organised by its star. Planets are subtopics, a dashed route leads to a neighbouring system, the station is a shared room. The list view replaces the rejected grey Worlds dialog.

Prototype · design data

Route & origin

/universe/ml from U01 system tap, a breadcrumb chip on an encounter, or Keep “Open in Universe”. ?view=list deep-links the outline.

Actions

Primary: tap a planet → U03 (camera dolly). Secondary: station → R01; dashed route → neighbouring system; List view; Lately lens; back pill to Universe. Dust is not tappable as a place.

States & overlays

established · forming (dashed slot + “Drawing…”, no percentage) · calm (−35% saturation, no glow, orbit 10%) · hidden/private boundary (fog, see U06) · list view sheet. Stale scene: keep old, swap when the new revision lands.

Commands · observations

OBS universe.select{planet} · universe.route{to} · universe.list_open
Scene revision fetched by id; client never computes eligibility or forms planets itself.

Restore

Camera {system, zoom, rotation}; list view scroll position and expanded planet. Returning from U03 animates back out to this exact framing.

Accessibility & platform

List view is the screen-reader primary: h2 system, h3 planets, 48px rows. Hit targets overlay every body. Esc / native back closes the sheet. Reduced motion: camera cuts with a 200ms fade.
