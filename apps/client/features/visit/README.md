# Friend visit (U06; #241, D-047)

`/visit/<handle>` shows a friend's universe: the systems and planets they chose to share, drawn by the
Universe's own renderer (`features/universe/`). The Friends screen's Visit button opens it by the friend's
public handle. Status marks: implemented with component tests over a labelled in-memory server; not
runtime-verified on dev.

| File                    | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

How it takes part in the journey: Friends row, then Visit, then one read (`POST /v1/social/visits/read`) that
the server checks again for friendship, blocks and the owner's audience. The screen rereads every 15 s while it
is open and the app is in the foreground, so an owner who stops sharing is noticed within that time, and it
drops the list after two minutes without an answer. Leaving, a refusal and the end of the account scope all
discard the list: it lives only in memory, so "nothing from it is kept on your side" is literally true. (With the new look the device keeps one thing about a visit: that this friend's crossing was shown, by public handle, as a convenience of the account on this device; see the crossing below. It is not the list, and it is purged with the account.)

**What we have in common** (Round 7.3, behind "Try the new Universe" only). At the whole-universe stop a chip dims the friend's systems whose names you do not have to 25 % light and their names to 0.4; the shared ones keep their light and wear a 2 px yellow ring (`CommonSky`, `universe/voyage/search-sky.tsx`, drawn under the names). While it is on, each shared system's button is named "<name>, a system, you have this too"; the chip is a button with `aria-pressed`. It is off when you arrive and when you visit another friend, hidden below the whole universe, and not shown when your own universe is empty or not in hand. Names are compared on this device; no read is added for it beyond your own sky read when the Universe tab has not loaded it, and no count is shown. Every system on your map counts, a hypothetical one included, as in search. In the new look the visit's map is pinned to the Light values like your own Universe (`printedSurface`), so it is the same in both appearances; the old look follows the appearance as before.

**The crossing** (Round 7.3, behind "Try the new Universe" only; `visit-crossing.tsx`, with the rules in `../universe/crossing/crossing-director.ts`). When `/visit/<handle>` first mounts, from a Friends row, the desktop chat rail or a link, the film plays full screen over the visit's loading, out, while the read runs behind it. The route has only the handle, so until the read gives the friend's name the film says "your friend's universe" and then the name; its one polite announcement ("Going to Ravi's universe") is made when the name is known or by film time 0.05, and never changes. The names you have in common (`systemsInCommon` over your own names) drift past only if the read and your own names were in hand at film time 0.47; later ones are left out and the film is never held for them. If the read says unavailable or the connection is lost before film time 0.3 (about 2.25 s of the first film), or the device already says it is offline, the film stops and the screens in `visit-screen.tsx` are shown; after 0.3 it finishes and they are under it. The flash ends over the friend's map, which is mounted behind the film from the start. It takes 7.5 s the first time for a friend on this device and 3.6 s after (`crossing-seen.ts`, written when a film ends or is skipped, never for a stopped film or a reduced-motion cut). A tap anywhere or Escape skips to film time 0.985 under a dark veil. With reduced motion there is no film, only a 0.26 s dark cut. Leave in the visitor bar plays the film home (3.8 s, "Going home", no names) and then goes to your Universe (`router.dismissTo('/universe')`: the visit is left, not stacked over, so Back does not return to it); "Back to Friends" and "Leave" on the not-shared and offline screens go back as before. With the switch off none of this is drawn or read: no film, no live region, no own-names read, and Leave goes back, or to Friends. The film's frames go to the frame-rate readout ("Show the Universe frame rate"): each draw, and the whole film as one flight, "crossing" (shown on the map's readout after it; the way home is not, as it ends in leaving). The film draws no initial; the bubbles are seeded from the handle. **The fall:** once the film hands over (it finished or was skipped; not a cut, not a stopped film), the visit gives the stage under it a fall (`FallInContext`, `../universe/voyage/fall-in.ts`): the camera starts seven times farther out than the friend's whole universe and falls to it with no ship and no flight line, 1.6 s the first time for a friend and about 0.9 s after (the prototype's `go`). A stage that mounts after the film (the read came late) falls on its first frame. The readout times it as the flight "fall". With the switch on the screen tells the map session its motion setting again once it is in front: the session ignores what it is told in the background, and it used to keep its opening "reduce motion", so every flight in a friend's Universe cut. With the switch off that is as it always was. Not built: the white of the way home fading over your own Universe (it needs a change in the Universe's own screen).

Not built: entering a room from a visit (this published view carries no room entry) and region cards (regions are never shared).
Visiting creates no evidence, observation or record for either person.

## Follow the request and check it

This feature uses the active account scope's client-core controller; the matching backend owner
checks the actual authority. Start with the [feature map](../README.md), then follow the screen's
session hook into [client-core](../../../../packages/client-core/README.md). A component fixture's
authored answer is not evidence that a live backend returned it.

From the repository root, run
`pnpm --filter @knowscroll/client exec jest --ci --runInBand features/visit`.
The app's hosted web smoke adds real browser/export checks. Keep privacy and failure assertions
intact; passing these checks does not establish a new dev or native-device acceptance.
